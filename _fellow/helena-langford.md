### Fellowship Bio
I am a Clinical Informatics Pharmacist, interested in responsible AI adoption to enhance patient care. The fellowship strengthened my experience in digital transformation and provided valuable insight into digital clinical safety and governance, guiding me to specialise further in these areas.

<!-- FELLOWSHIP_PROJECT -->
Preparing for outpatient appointments can be time consuming and contribute to clinician administrative burden. This can reduce time available for direct patient care and impact clinician wellbeing. Epic’s Outpatient Insights summarisation tool uses the GPT 4.1 large language model (LLM) to generate concise patient history summaries from electronic patient record notes, reducing the need for manual review. The overall goal of the project was to support deployment of this tool, dependent on a clinical evaluation of summary quality, followed by a pilot within gastroenterology and healthcare for older people outpatient clinics. My role involved working closely with the multidisciplinary project team to drive delivery and acting as project support lead for the clinical evaluation of LLM generated summaries. Regarding the clinical evaluation, I supported preliminary testing of the tool, designed the clinical evaluation protocol, drove delivery through clinician recruitment and clinical evaluation workshops, and supported data analysis. I also contributed to wider deployment planning, reviewing compliance with regulations, progressing work on the clinical safety case and facilitating a hazard workshop, and supporting with plans for the pilot phase and ongoing monitoring. Having completed data collection for the clinical evaluation, we are currently performing data analysis. Review of the results of the clinical evaluation and completion of the clinical safety case will inform the decision to proceed with a pilot. A 12-week pilot is planned in gastroenterology and healthcare for older people to assess impacts on efficiency and clinician wellbeing, supporting decisions regarding wider deployment.
