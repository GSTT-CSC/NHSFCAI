### Fellowship Bio
I am a Saudi physician and a graduate of King Saud University. I am an NHS Fellow in Clinical AI and a Geriatric Medicine Fellow at Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust, London.

<!-- FELLOWSHIP_PROJECT -->
The project aimed to develop a system-wide framework to support the safe, ethical, consistent, and effective use of artificial intelligence (AI) across the South East London Integrated Care System (SEL ICS). Its goal was to enable the rapid and responsible adoption of AI while ensuring that patient safety, NHS standards, ethics, regulation, and good governance remained central to decision-making. The project also provided oversight, coordination, and expert advice for AI-related projects. My main contribution was as an active member of the project team, attending meetings with key stakeholders and learning how organisations approach AI adoption and governance in real-world healthcare settings. I developed practical skills in conducting AI maturity assessments, including using the assessment framework, interpreting responses, and categorising organisations according to their level of maturity. I also gained experience in understanding AI roadmaps, regulation, and governance. A key milestone was gaining hands-on experience in assessing organisational AI maturity and applying theoretical knowledge in practice. This helped me understand the practical challenges of implementing AI safely and showed me that real-world projects often involve unexpected issues that cannot always be anticipated through theory alone. The project is due to finish in August, although monitoring and support will continue. Further work will include helping organisations address identified gaps and ensuring that the framework remains practical and aligned with evolving NHS AI standards. Overall, the project has strengthened my understanding of AI governance and provided valuable practical experience in supporting safe and scalable AI adoption in healthcare.

### Fellowship Testimonial
When I first decided to study Artificial Intelligence, my mother joked that it would help compensate for my natural stupidity. Looking back, I have to admit—she was absolutely right. Over the past year, I have experienced remarkable personal and professional growth. AI has not only changed my understanding of technology; it has changed the way I think, approach challenges, and see the world. This journey taught me that AI is about far more than algorithms and data. It is about curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning. One of the most rewarding parts of the fellowship has been meeting inspiring people from different backgrounds, connecting with pioneers in the field, and learning alongside brilliant fellows. Sharing ideas and exploring new perspectives has been just as valuable as the technical knowledge itself. The fellowship has been exceptionally well organised, with approachable and supportive people throughout. The workshops held across England provided a unique experience, allowing us to learn from different environments and understand how ideas can be applied in real-world settings. I have always been an indecisive person, but choosing to pursue Clinical AI is one decision I have never regretted. It has challenged and inspired me, while opening doors I never knew existed. As I graduate, I am grateful to my family, mentors, colleagues, and fellow fellows for making this journey unforgettable. This is not the end—it is just the beginning.
