### Fellowship Bio
I am a public health doctor completing my specialty training in London. I am particularly interested in deploying AI safely within the NHS and have experience using data, digital and AI innovation to improve health.

<!-- FELLOWSHIP_PROJECT -->
My project focused on MIDI, an AI tool which supports radiologists to identify clinically significant abnormalities on MRI brain scans. Where configured, MIDI allows abnormal scans to be prioritised within radiology worklists, so they can be reviewed more quickly. My role was to work with the multidisciplinary team of clinicians, academics, software engineers, project manager and quality specialists to develop clinical safety documentation and prepare MIDI for real-world deployment within live clinical workflows. In particular, I led finalisation of the Clinical Evaluation Report, development of the Risk Management Report and establishment of post market surveillance of the AI tool. My role involved developing DCB0129 documentation for the AI device with the wider team, including leading on the Clinical Safety Case Report. I also supported implementation of MIDI across different NHS trusts. Key milestones included achieving UKCA Class I AI medical device marking, completion of key safety and regulatory information, and important progress for deployment at different NHS trusts. The next phase of the project is a cluster-randomised cross-over trial designed to evaluate the clinical effectiveness, cost-effectiveness, acceptability, safety, and impact on clinical outcomes of MIDI.
