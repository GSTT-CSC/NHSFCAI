title: Sponsor an NHS Applicant
description: How NHS organisations can sponsor a post on the NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI.
---

Sponsoring a post lets you nominate an NHS applicant for the fellowship. A **nominated applicant** enters the programme non-competitively — they are not ranked against other applicants for a place — but must still meet the Essential criteria of the Person Specification at interview. This is distinct from the **competitive** nationally- and regionally-funded routes.

For Cohort 6, sponsors must confirm funding to the faculty by **11 Dec 2026**. Bespoke arrangements can be made in addition to the documentation below.

Prospective sponsors should [contact the faculty](mailto:gstt.aifellowship@nhs.net) directly.
