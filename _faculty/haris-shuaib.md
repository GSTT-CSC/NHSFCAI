### Bio
Haris Shuaib, PhD, founded the NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI and continues to act as a key strategic advisor. He is the former head of [GSTT Clinical Scientific Computing](https://gstt-csc.github.io) which he led from its creation in 2021 until 2024. 
Haris is the CEO of [Newton's Tree](https://www.newtonstree.ai).
