### Bio
Dr Alexander T Deng a medical Consultant in Genomic AI.
He has developed [auditable standards for AI in healthcare](https://standardsdevelopment.bsigroup.com/projects/2021-00605) with the British Standards Institution as an editorial panel chair. 
Alexander is the Director of the [NHS Genomic AI Network](https://genomicainetwork.nhs.uk), and the genomic AI theme lead in the [Alan Turing Institute Clinical AI Interest Group](https://www.turing.ac.uk/research/interest-groups/clinical-ai). 


### Team Role
Alexander is the [Director of the NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI](https://web.archive.org/web/20260630032111/https://www.kingshealthpartners.org/latest/dr-alexander-deng-profile), and author of the accompanying [curriculum](/assets/docs/FCAI_Curriculum_v3.2.pdf). This unique programme equips NHS healthcare leaders for clinical AI transformation. Alexander was awarded a [Topol Digital Fellowship](https://web.archive.org/web/20250910182334/https://topol.hee.nhs.uk/digital-fellowships/fellows/alexander-deng/) to deliver this programme across the NHS.
