The NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI is a 12-month programme integrated part-time alongside clinical work. Fellows are recruited from a diverse clinical workforce, and gain experience deploying and evaluating AI in clinical workflows under expert supervision in multidisciplinary NHS teams.

Our fellows are smart and energetic healthcare leaders who are helping to make the NHS the most AI-enabled workforce in the world by 2035.
